# Go-to-Market Plan

> **Status:** Draft · **Owner:** Marketing Lead · **Audience:** Marketing, sales, leadership

## 1. Ideal customer profile (ICP)

- 10–200 employees
- Holds physical stock (retail, wholesale, distribution, light manufacturing), or professional services with billing needs
- Uses an accounting app plus spreadsheets today
- Has a finance person and an operations person
- Located in launch market **[TBD]**

**Buying trigger examples:** opening a second branch or warehouse, failed audit, stock-out losses, hiring a finance manager, outgrowing an accounting app.

## 3. Launch phases

| Phase | Goal | Activities |
|---|---|---|
| Pre-launch (Discovery/MVP) | Validate problem and pricing; build waitlist | Customer interviews, landing page, waitlist, founder-led content |
| Pilot (MVP/Beta) | 10–20 paying pilots, case studies | Hand-held onboarding, weekly feedback calls, discounted pilot pricing |
| Launch (GA) | Public awareness and pipeline | Launch event/webinar, PR, partner announcements, paid campaigns |
| Growth | Repeatable acquisition | SEO content, referrals, accountant/consultant partner program |

## 4. Channels

| Channel | Why | KPI |
|---|---|---|
| SEO content (guides, templates, comparisons) | SMBs search for "inventory spreadsheet template", "how to do month-end close" | Organic signups |
| Accountant and bookkeeper partners | Trusted advisors recommend software to clients | Partner-sourced tenants |
| Webinars and demos | ERP is a considered purchase | Demo-to-trial conversion |
| Paid search and social | Capture high-intent demand | Cost per trial |
| Referrals | Happy customers know similar businesses | Referral signups |
| Industry associations and events | Reach ICP directly | Leads per event |
