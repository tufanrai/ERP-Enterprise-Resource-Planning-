## Categories of competition

| Category | Examples | Their strength | Our angle |
|---|---|---|---|
| Large enterprise ERP | SAP S/4HANA, Oracle NetSuite, Microsoft Dynamics 365 | Depth, brand, enterprise features | Too costly and slow for SMBs; we're faster and cheaper to adopt |
| SMB ERP suites | SAP Business One, Dynamics 365 Business Central, Odoo, ERPNext | Broad modules | Often need partners/consultants to implement; we focus on self-serve setup |
| Accounting apps | QuickBooks, Xero, Zoho Books | Easy, cheap, popular | Limited inventory, purchasing and HR; customers outgrow them |
| Point tools + spreadsheets | Separate stock, CRM and payroll apps | Familiar, low cost | Disconnected data; we unify it |

## Our differentiators (to validate)

1. **Self-serve onboarding** with industry templates (go live in days).
2. **Modular pricing**: pay only for modules used.
3. **Local fit**: tax, currency and language for our launch market **[TBD]**.
4. **Modern UX** designed for non-accountants.
5. **Responsive local support** during onboarding.
