# Positioning and Messaging

> **Status:** Draft · **Owner:** Marketing Lead · **Audience:** Marketing, sales, founders, customer success

## 1. Positioning statement

**For** growing small and mid-sized businesses **who** have outgrown spreadsheets and basic accounting apps, **[Product name]** is a **cloud ERP** that **connects finance, inventory, sales, purchasing and HR in one place**. **Unlike** traditional ERP systems that need consultants, servers and months of setup, **we** get a business running in days, at a monthly price that grows with them.

## 2. Tagline options (to test)

1. "Run your whole business from one place."
2. "The ERP you can set up this week."
3. "Grow without changing systems."

## 3. Core message pillars

| Pillar | Message | Proof points |
|---|---|---|
| **All-in-one** | Finance, stock, sales, buying and people in one system, so everyone sees the same numbers | Integrated modules; one login; real-time dashboards |
| **Fast to start** | Go live in days, not months, without consultants | Setup wizard, industry templates, spreadsheet import |
| **Grows with you** | Start with one module, add more, add branches and currencies when you need them | Modular pricing, multi-company, multi-warehouse, multi-currency |
| **Trustworthy** | Accurate books and secure data | Double-entry accounting, full audit trail, encryption, role-based access, 99.9% uptime target |
| **Connected** | Works with the tools you already use | Open API, webhooks, payment and e-commerce integrations |

## 4. Messages by audience

| Audience | They care about | Lead with |
|---|---|---|
| Business owner | Visibility, cost, growth | "See cash, sales and stock in real time, without waiting for reports." |
| Finance manager | Accuracy, close speed, compliance | "Close the month in days. Every number traceable to its source." |
| Operations manager | Stock accuracy, fewer stock-outs | "Know what you have, where it is, and when to reorder." |
| Sales team | Speed, stock visibility | "Quote and confirm orders in minutes with live stock levels." |
| IT / admin | Security, integration, low maintenance | "No servers. SSO, roles, audit logs and an open API." |

## 5. Elevator pitches

**10 seconds:** "We're a cloud ERP that runs a growing company's finance, stock, sales and HR in one place, and you can set it up in a week."

**30 seconds:** "Most growing businesses juggle spreadsheets, an accounting app and a stock tool that don't talk to each other. Traditional ERP fixes that but costs a fortune and takes months. We give them one cloud system, priced per user per month, that they can set up themselves in days. They start with what they need and add modules as they grow."

## 6. Words to use and avoid

| Use | Avoid |
|---|---|
| "Set up in days" | "Implementation project" |
| "One place for everything" | "Synergistic platform" |
| "Your numbers, in real time" | Technical jargon (multi-tenant, RLS, microservices) in customer copy |
| Concrete outcomes (hours saved, fewer stock-outs) | Unverified claims ("#1", "best") |

## 7. Brand voice

Clear, confident, practical. Talk like a helpful operations expert, not a software salesperson.
