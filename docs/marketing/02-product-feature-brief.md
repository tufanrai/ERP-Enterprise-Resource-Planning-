# Product Feature Brief (for Marketing and Sales)

> **Status:** Draft · **Owner:** Product Marketing · **Audience:** Marketing, sales, customer success
> Technical detail lives in the [Feature catalog](../product/04-feature-catalog.md). This page translates features into benefits.

## Module summaries

### Finance & Accounting
**Benefit:** Accurate books and faster month-end close.
- Professional invoices emailed in one click
- Know who owes you and whom you owe, with aging reports
- Tax calculated automatically
- Profit & loss, balance sheet and cash flow any time
- Bank reconciliation and multi-currency (Beta)

### Inventory
**Benefit:** Fewer stock-outs, less dead stock.
- Stock across every warehouse in real time
- Automatic reorder alerts
- Accurate stock valuation that flows into the books
- Batch and serial tracking (Beta)

### Sales
**Benefit:** Faster quotes, faster cash.
- Quote → order → delivery → invoice without retyping
- Live stock availability while quoting
- Customer-specific prices and discounts (Beta)

### Purchasing (Beta)
**Benefit:** Control spend and pay only for what arrived.
- Purchase approvals
- Purchase orders and partial receipts
- Automatic matching of order, receipt and bill

### HR & Payroll (GA)
**Benefit:** Payroll on time without spreadsheets.
- Employee records, leave and attendance
- Payroll runs and payslips
- Self-service for employees

### Reporting & Dashboards
**Benefit:** Decisions based on today's numbers.
- Role-based dashboards
- Standard reports with export to PDF, Excel and CSV
- Scheduled report emails

### Platform
**Benefit:** Secure, connected, and easy to manage.
- Roles and permissions, full audit trail
- Two-factor authentication and single sign-on
- Open API and webhooks
- Import from spreadsheets in minutes

## Proof points to collect during beta

- Time from signup to first invoice
- Hours saved at month-end
- Stock accuracy improvement
- Customer quotes and case studies (with permission)

## Frequently asked sales questions

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Do I need to install anything? | No. It runs in the browser. |
| Can I move my existing data? | Yes. Import customers, products, suppliers and opening balances from spreadsheets. |
| Is my data safe? | Encrypted in transit and at rest, daily backups, role-based access and a full audit trail. |
| Can I start small? | Yes. Start with one module and add more anytime. |
| Does it work on my phone? | Yes, through the mobile browser. Native apps are planned. |
| Can it connect to my other tools? | Yes, through our API and webhooks; ready-made integrations are being added. |
