# Feature Catalog

> **Status:** Draft · **Owner:** Product Lead · **Audience:** Support, sales, marketing, QA, new team members

A plain-language list of what the product does, by module. Use it to answer "can the product do X?". Requirement IDs link back to the [PRD](01-product-requirements.md).

| Module | Feature | What it does for the user | Phase | PRD ID |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Core | Guided setup | Creates a ready-to-use company in minutes from an industry template | MVP | CORE-01 |
| Core | Roles and permissions | Controls who can see and do what, per module | MVP | CORE-04 |
| Core | Audit trail | Shows who changed what and when, for every record | MVP | CORE-06 |
| Core | Import / export | Bring data in from spreadsheets; export any list | MVP | CORE-09 |
| Core | Approvals | Routes purchases, expenses or discounts for approval | Beta | CORE-11 |
| Core | SSO | Sign in with the company's identity provider | GA | CORE-03 |
| Finance | Invoicing | Create and email professional invoices | MVP | FIN-03 |
| Finance | Payments | Track who has paid and who owes | MVP | FIN-05 |
| Finance | Tax | Calculates tax automatically and produces tax reports | MVP | FIN-06 |
| Finance | Financial reports | P&L, balance sheet, cash flow, aging | MVP | FIN-09 |
| Finance | Bank reconciliation | Match bank statement lines to transactions | Beta | FIN-08 |
| Finance | Multi-currency | Sell and buy in foreign currencies | Beta | FIN-07 |
| Inventory | Multi-warehouse | Track stock in every location | MVP | INV-02 |
| Inventory | Stock valuation | Know the value of stock at any time | MVP | INV-04 |
| Inventory | Reorder alerts | Get warned before running out | MVP | INV-05 |
| Inventory | Lot / serial tracking | Trace items by batch or serial number | Beta | INV-06 |
| Sales | Quotes to orders | Turn a quote into an order in one click | MVP | SAL-02 |
| Sales | Price lists | Different prices for different customers | Beta | SAL-05 |
| Purchasing | Purchase orders | Order from suppliers and track receipts | Beta | PUR-03 |
| Purchasing | Three-way match | Pay only for what was ordered and received | Beta | PUR-04 |
| HR | Leave management | Request and approve leave online | GA | HR-02 |
| HR | Payroll | Calculate pay and issue payslips | GA | HR-04 |
| Reporting | Dashboards | Key numbers on the home screen per role | MVP | REP-01 |
| Integrations | API and webhooks | Connect other apps to the ERP | Beta | INT-01, INT-02 |
