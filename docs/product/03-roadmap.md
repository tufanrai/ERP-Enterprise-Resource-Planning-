# Product Roadmap

> **Status:** Draft · **Owner:** Product Lead · **Audience:** All teams, stakeholders
> Dates are targets, not commitments, until the charter is approved.

## Phases

### Phase 0: Discovery and foundations — [TBD]
- Customer interviews (at least 15 target businesses)
- PRD, architecture and ADRs signed off
- Design system and key wireframes
- Repository, CI/CD, environments, observability baseline

### Phase 1: MVP — [TBD]
- Platform core: tenants, users, RBAC, audit log, settings, import/export
- Finance: chart of accounts, journals, invoices, bills, payments, tax, core reports
- Inventory: products, warehouses, stock movements, valuation, reorder alerts
- Sales: customers, quotes, orders, deliveries, invoicing
- Basic dashboards
- **Exit criteria:** 3–5 pilot tenants running order-to-cash end to end

### Phase 2: Beta — [TBD]
- Purchasing module
- Subscription billing and self-serve signup
- Bank reconciliation, multi-currency
- Public API v1 and webhooks
- Approval workflows
- **Exit criteria:** 10–20 paying tenants, 99.9% uptime for 60 days

### Phase 3: General availability — [TBD]
- HR & Payroll for launch market
- Advanced reporting and scheduled reports
- SSO (SAML/OIDC)
- Security assessment passed
- Public marketing launch

### Later (post-GA backlog)
- Mobile apps
- Manufacturing (bill of materials, work orders)
- Marketplace of integrations
- AI assistance (forecasting, anomaly detection, natural-language reports)
- Additional countries' tax and payroll packs
